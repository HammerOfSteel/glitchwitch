# Character, NPC & Creature Inventory

Full roster of every character, NPC, animal, and creature planned for Glitch Witch —
the source list for `docs/character-art-prompts.md` (Meshy AI generation prompts).
Complements `docs/asset-inventory.md` (which tracks asset *pipeline* status per row);
this document is the *casting* reference — who exists, what they look like, why they're
in the game.

**No combat, ever** (design-bible.md pillar 1: "mending instead of fighting"). There are
no enemies in the traditional sense. Where "enemy"-adjacent content exists, it's
**glitch fauna** — anomaly creatures you *observe and mend* with Witch Sight, never
fight. That section exists to answer "what would an enemy inventory look like here."

Status legend: **existing** = named in `docs/story-bible.md` or `docs/asset-inventory.md`
already. **proposed** = new, added in this pass per the owner's "more is better" note —
not yet in the story bible; treat as a casting suggestion pending owner veto (story bible
rule: "all names below are proposals — the owner has veto on everything").

Body-type legend (drives which rig archetype + reference-sheet pose is needed):
**humanoid** (T-pose turnaround) · **child** (humanoid, smaller proportions, T-pose) ·
**quadruped** (4-point stance turnaround, no T-pose) · **bird** (wings-spread turnaround)
· **small-creature** (bespoke pose per design, noted per row).

## 6. Glitch fauna — anomaly creatures (proposed; the "enemy" answer for a non-combat game)

**Design rule (binding, per design-bible.md's "glitch vocabulary" table):** every anomaly
type has exactly one visual verb (Loop, Lag, Dupe, Seam, Missing). Glitch fauna are
**ordinary village animals mid-anomaly**, not separate monster designs — this keeps the
"the world glitches, gently" tone and avoids inventing a bestiary that reads as combat
enemies. Each row below is an *anomaly skin/pose*, not a new base creature model, except
where noted. None are fought; all are approached, observed with Witch Sight, and mended.
